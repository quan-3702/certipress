CertiPress – How to use

Make and print award certificates, diplomas and gift certificates – one for each name on your list.

1. Choose a design

  1. Click one of the eight small pictures under Design. The big preview on the right changes straight away.
  2. Click a coloured circle to change the main colour.
  3. Pick your Paper (US Letter or A4) and the Direction: wide or tall.
  4. Lettering changes the style of the letters. "Classic with handwriting name" writes the name in a handwriting style.

2. Write the words

  1. Title: click the box and pick a ready-made title, such as Certificate of Appreciation, or type your own.
  2. Line above the name: for example "This certificate is proudly presented to".
  3. What it is for: one or two sentences. Long text is wrapped onto several lines by itself.
Not sure where to begin? Open ☰ More and choose Fill in an example, then change the words.

3. Make one certificate for each person

  1. In Names, type one name on each line.
  2. You get one certificate for every name. Use the ‹ and › buttons beside the preview to look at each one.
  3. A very long name is made a little smaller so that it always fits.
Leave the Names box empty to print a certificate with an empty line. You can then write the name by hand.

4. Date, signatures and seal

  • Show the date: untick it to leave the date out. You can type the date any way you like.
  • Signed by and Their role: the name and role are printed under a line, so the person can sign above it with a pen.
  • Second signature: fill it in only if two people sign.
  • Show the gold seal: untick it if you prefer a plain certificate.

5. Print

  1. Click 🖨 Print at the top.
  2. Choose your printer. The paper size and direction are set for you.
  3. Set Margins to None and Scale to 100% if the print window offers these choices.
  4. Thicker paper (card stock, 160–200 g/m²) looks best.
To make a PDF file instead of paper, choose Save as PDF or Microsoft Print to PDF as the printer.

6. Add your logo (Pro)

  1. Click Add a logo picture… and choose a picture file from your PC.
  2. The logo appears at the top, in the middle.
  3. Click Remove logo to take it away again.

7. Save as a picture (Pro)

  1. Show the certificate you want with the ‹ and › buttons.
  2. Open ☰ More and choose Save this certificate as a picture…
  3. A PNG picture is saved in your Downloads folder, named after the person. You can send it by email or message.

8. Free version and Pro

The free version never limits how often you print.

  • Free: 3 designs, up to 5 names at a time, all colours, both paper sizes.
  • Pro (one-time purchase): all 8 designs, unlimited names at a time, your own logo, save as a picture.

You can look at every design in the preview before you decide.

9. If something goes wrong

  • The border is cut off at the edges: most printers cannot print right to the edge of the paper. Set Margins to None, or choose "Fit to page" in the print window.
  • An empty second page comes out: set Scale to 100% and Margins to None.
  • The colours are missing: turn on Background graphics in the print window, under More settings.
  • My texts are gone on another PC: they are stored only on the PC where you typed them.
